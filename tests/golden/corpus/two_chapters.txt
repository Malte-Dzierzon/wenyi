# Chapter One

The old lighthouse stood at the edge of the cliff. Every evening, keeper Elias
Vance lit the great lamp and watched the ships pass below.

“Another storm coming,” he muttered, tightening his coat against the wind.

# Chapter Two

In the village, baker Anna Berger prepared bread for the morning market.
Her daughter Ann helped shape the loaves while the ovens roared.
